The Family Float

Perfect for families with children or first-timers. This trip takes you through the scenic lower canyon where the water is calm but the views are spectacular. Includes a riverside lunch and swimming breaks.

The Canyon Challenge

Our most popular trip! Navigate through Class III rapids that provide plenty of splashes and excitement without being too overwhelming. A great mix of paddling action and relaxing drift sections.

The Thunder Run

For adrenaline junkies only. Challenge the Class IV and V rapids of the upper river. Previous rafting experience is recommended. This full-day adventure will test your stamina and teamwork.
